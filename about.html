About me

Hello! I'm Beatriz, an aspiring UX/Ui Designer in Lisbon, Portugal, looking to start my career!

I have a masters in Fine Arts, which gave me great understanding about visual culture, and thought me how to research.

In 2021 I started studying UX/UI, and recently finished a course in FLAG, Lisbon. This consolidated my knowledge and was an opportunity to gain hands on experience.

In the past few years I also worked as a freelance illustrator, creating album cover art. I love creating illustrations and painting, and have a passion for psychology and understanding human behavior.

With my work, I want to create designs that solve the users real problems in a holistic way.

Currently im looking to start a career in UX/UI, expand my knowledge, and work on challenging projects in a group setting.
